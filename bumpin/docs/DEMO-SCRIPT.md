# Three-minute demo — Love at First Bot, by BumpIn

For the exact taps, screens and fallbacks, follow [DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md](DEMO-WALKTHROUGH.md).

Use two consenting people and a laptop. Rehearse the complete flow before presenting. Fictional demo profiles must be labelled as fictional; don't describe browser preview tabs as separate phones.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem and the joke

“Developers need designers. Designers need developers. Somehow, they spend the whole evening asking each other where the pizza is. We built BumpIn's Love at First Bot to make that first useful introduction.”

Name the real community using the demo. Explain that profiles are volunteered and self-declared.

## 0:20–0:50 — Two people join

Show `/qr`. Tap **Join with LinkedIn** if the provider has been configured and rehearsed. After LinkedIn authorization the event opens automatically; sharing is explained on the action. While LinkedIn is disabled, use guest entry. Both entry routes appear on the map automatically, with Hide/Leave available afterward. Open **Find my collaborator**: Phone A chooses **Build apps** and needs **Design**; Phone B chooses **Design** and needs **Build apps**. No GPS is requested. Do not claim LinkedIn imports skills or work history.

## 0:50–1:25 — Evidence, then an invitation

Open Find my collaborator. Show both directions of the match and the exact profile excerpts. There is no invented percentage and no external AI call. Phone A requests an introduction. Phone B taps Let’s connect.

## 1:25–1:50 — Something useful happens

Show the opener and the suggested ten-minute experiment. Ask the two participants whether it is relevant. Don't claim they collaborated merely because they accepted.

## 1:50–2:35 — The show

Both open Take this match to the big screen. Optionally select a curated personal joke. Each reviews the exact preview and approves it. Copy the available stage link to the laptop. Click Reveal the match.

“One useful introduction. Two yeses. Finally, a relationship with a next step.”

## 2:35–3:00 — Privacy and the close

Revoke stage permission on either phone. Within the next successful permission check the stage clears. Show the private introduction still exists.

“Less small talk. More shipping. Built for the people already in your community.”

Close with the public repository URL only once publication is verified. Mention MIT, setup instructions, and that another community can run it. Keep a clearly labelled backup recording in case the venue network fails.
